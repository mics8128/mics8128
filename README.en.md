## Engineering Philosophy

I care about systems that are useful, maintainable, and actually adopted by users.

My approach:

1. Understand the real workflow before writing code
2. Build the smallest useful version first
3. Connect systems instead of forcing people to copy-paste data
4. Use AI where it creates leverage, not just novelty
5. Keep humans in the loop when judgment matters
6. Automate repeated work, document edge cases, and improve over time

Good software is not just about clean code.  
It is also about understanding people, workflows, constraints, and long-term maintenance.

---

## How I Think About AI

I see AI as a practical layer that can improve business systems when it is used carefully.

For me, AI is most useful when it can:

- Reduce repetitive writing or classification work
- Help people retrieve internal knowledge faster
- Draft structured responses
- Compare and summarize information
- Connect workflows across tools
- Assist humans without removing accountability

I prefer AI systems that are:

- Transparent
- Reviewable
- Connected to real workflows
- Designed with escalation paths
- Easy to improve over time
